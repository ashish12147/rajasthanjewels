THE PRODUCT VISION

Artistry meets intelligence.

We envision a more useful jewellery exploration platform: one that helps people discover styles, articulate preferences and understand heritage craft, with clear explanations rather than black-box suggestions.

A TRANSPARENT DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

The roadmap, not the hype.

Phase 01 / Now

Public prototype

Responsive site, design-system foundation, concept catalogue, local saved items and a working style quiz. All client-side; no accounts or payment processing.

Phase 02 / Planned

Claude concierge

Introduce a secured server-side Anthropic API integration for conversational styling, multilingual assistance, and explanations. API credentials would never be exposed in the browser.

Phase 03 / Future

Real catalogue

Integrate authorised supplier information, accurate product photography, inventory management and transaction infrastructure—only after sourcing and compliance are in place.

TECHNICAL PRINCIPLE

Design for trust,
build for change.

Our first version is intentionally simple: a fast, accessible static frontend, original concept data, browser-only favourites and an explainable recommendation algorithm. No personal account or behavioural tracking is required to try the experience.
