INFORMATION & PRIVACY

Simple by design.

What this prototype does—and doesn't—collect.

Last updated: October 8, 2026. This policy describes the Rajasthan Jewels concept-stage website. It will be updated when new services are introduced.

1. Information stored on your device

Saved items are stored locally in your browser using localStorage under the key rj-saved. The style quiz works in your browser and does not transmit your answers to any server. You can clear saved items using your browser's site-data controls.

2. Contact form

The contact form prepares an email using your device's email client. It does not directly send the information to our servers or store form submissions. Any email you choose to send will be handled by your email provider and the recipient's provider.

3. Third-party resources

The site uses Google Fonts and Unsplash-hosted imagery. Loading those resources may share technical request information such as your IP address with the respective providers under their own privacy practices. The site does not implement Google Analytics, advertising trackers or remarketing tags.

4. Payments and accounts

This prototype has no account creation, checkout, payment collection or order processing. Concept items are not available for purchase.

5. Future updates

If we later introduce a Claude-powered assistant, user accounts or mailing-list sign-up, we will revise this policy and disclose what information is transmitted and retained before activating those services.

6. Questions

For questions about the project or this policy, use the contact page. The proposed domain mailbox requires configuration before it can receive email.
